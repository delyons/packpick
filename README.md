# PackPick

### Find a meal plan and a place to live that fit your life at NC State.

PackPick helps NC State students explore meal-plan and housing options through short, personalized quizzes. See recommendations based on your situation, compare the details, and keep your picks handy in one place.

> **A note about PackPick:** This is an independent student project and is not affiliated with or operated by NC State University. Information and prices can change. Confirm eligibility, costs, and other details with NC State before making decisions.

## How to use PackPick

1. **Set up your profile.** Choose your year and indicate whether you are a roster athlete. This helps tailor quiz questions and show options that may apply to you.
2. **Choose a quiz.** Take the meal-plan quiz, the housing quiz, or both. Questions adapt to your answers, so each quiz takes only as long as needed.
3. **Review your recommendation.** Explore the result and its relevant details, then save it to your profile.
4. **Manage your picks.** Open **Your Profile** to revisit or change saved options, or choose a meal plan or housing option yourself.

## Explore your options

### Meal plans

Explore Flex, Block, Commuter, and athletic plans. Recommendations take your profile, eating habits, and weekly Dining Dollars budget into account. Results show meal-plan and Dining Dollar details, including an estimated top-up when applicable.

### Housing

Explore residence halls, apartments, off-campus options, and living-learning villages. Housing questions adapt to your year and preferences to help narrow down the options.

## Your profile and quiz history

- Save a recommended meal plan or housing option—or choose one yourself.
- Update or remove saved picks whenever you like.
- Review recent quiz answers and results, reopen a result, or retake a quiz.
- Clear your recent quiz history from the home page.

Your profile and quiz history are saved in your browser on this device. PackPick does not require an account, and saved information is not synced across devices or browsers.

## Information and pricing

Meal-plan prices shown are 2026–27 per-semester rates, before North Carolina sales tax. Housing information and projected rates are for 2026–27; the listed housing total includes the $150 ResNet fee. Check the official sources for the latest information:

- [NC State Dining meal plans](https://dining.ncsu.edu/meal-plans/)
- [NC State University Housing](https://housing.dasa.ncsu.edu/)
- [University Housing costs](https://housing.dasa.ncsu.edu/find-a-community/costs/)
